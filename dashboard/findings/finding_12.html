EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · MEASURING THIRD-PARTY NUMERICAL PRESENCE

This investigation evaluates the constituency-level relationship between certified electoral victory margins and the certified vote totals secured by Naam Tamilar Katchi (NTK) in the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly election. Across all 234 assembly divisions, this analytical model identifies every constituency where the NTK vote total surpassed the certified vote gap separating the winning candidate from the runner-up.

The objective of this analysis is strictly mathematical: to measure the numerical footprint of a non-aligned third-party vote bank across legislative contests. It does not predict hypothetical election outcomes, simulate alternative voting scenarios, or assume voter transfer behaviors in counterfactual environments.

SECTION 1 · KEY STATISTICAL FINDINGS

Out of 234 assembly constituencies in Tamil Nadu, NTK's certified vote total exceeded the winning margin in exactly 89 constituencies, representing 38.03% of all legislative divisions across the state.

The largest numerical gap between NTK's vote tally and the winning margin occurred in Manamadurai, where the certified victory margin stood at 1,208 votes while NTK polled 19,676 votes—a net excess of 18,468 votes.

The most dramatic example of this relationship was recorded in Tiruppattur, where the certified winning margin was a single vote (1 vote) while the NTK candidate secured 15,291 votes.

SECTION 2 · WHAT THE NUMBERS SHOW

In 89 constituencies across Tamil Nadu, the total number of electors who cast certified ballots for NTK was larger than the net margin separating the winner from the first runner-up. This mathematical condition highlights the substantial size of NTK's voting base relative to local contest margins.

Rather than remaining concentrated in a small number of pockets, NTK established a numerical vote share that surpassed victory margins across diverse geographical zones—including northern agricultural belts, western industrial corridors, central delta districts, and southern coastal divisions.

SECTION 5 · STATISTICAL INTERPRETATION

It is critical to interpret these mathematical findings with complete analytical rigor. The fact that NTK's vote total exceeded the victory margin in 89 constituencies does not imply that election outcomes would have differed under hypothetical alternative scenarios.

Certified election returns record actual voter choices cast under specific multi-candidate conditions. They cannot predict how voters would behave if candidate lists or alliance structures were altered. This analysis establishes only that NTK maintained a vote total larger than the certified margin of victory in 38.03% of assembly divisions.

METHODOLOGY · ECI VERIFIED COMPUTATION
Data Source: Official Election Commission of India (ECI) certified assembly returns (2026).

Method: For every one of Tamil Nadu's 234 assembly constituencies:
1. Obtain the certified winning margin (Votes for Winner minus Votes for 1st Runner-Up).
2. Obtain the certified total votes polled by the Naam Tamilar Katchi (NTK) candidate.
3. Compare the two values.
4. Mark the constituency if NTK Total Votes > Certified Winning Margin.
5. Sum all qualifying constituencies.

Verified Result: Exactly 89 of 234 constituencies (38.03%) satisfy the condition.
The distribution of these 89 constituencies across multiple regions indicates that the phenomenon was not confined to a single district or political stronghold. Instead, it reflects the statewide numerical presence of NTK's vote base during the 2026 Assembly election.
Data Quality Note: All figures are based on certified Election Commission of India results. Calculations were performed using the BigQuery dataset (fact_results_2026 and fact_margins_2026) and verified across all 234 constituencies.
