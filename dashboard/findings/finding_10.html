EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · CONSTITUTIONAL RESERVATION MANDATE

Constitutional reservation sets aside constituencies to ensure opportunities for representation of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly. In Tamil Nadu, constitutional provisions set aside specific assembly constituencies to preserve diverse political voices inside the state legislature.

Out of 234 total assembly constituencies in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly, exactly 46 constituencies are constitutionally reserved, comprising 44 Scheduled Caste reserved seats and 2 Scheduled Tribe reserved seats. Reserved constituencies account for 19.7% of Tamil Nadu's 234-member Legislative Assembly.

The 2026 election demonstrates how different political parties performed across these constitutionally designated constituencies. Certified election results confirm that Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) secured the highest number of Scheduled Caste reserved seats, establishing a strong presence across reserved legislative territories.

SECTION 1 · ASSEMBLY COMPOSITION

Nearly four-fifths of Tamil Nadu's Assembly constituencies are General constituencies, while 46 constituencies are reserved under constitutional provisions to ensure legislative representation for Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe communities. Reserved constituencies account for 19.7% of Tamil Nadu's 234-member Legislative Assembly.

Out of the 234 total assembly constituencies, 188 constituencies are General category seats, 44 constituencies are Scheduled Caste (SC) reserved, and 2 constituencies are Scheduled Tribe (ST) reserved seats.

SECTION 2 · PARTY PERFORMANCE IN SC RESERVED CONSTITUENCIES

Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) won more than half of all SC reserved constituencies, securing 23 out of the 44 available seats (52.3% of all SC seats). TVK won 23 SC reserved constituencies, exceeding the combined total of DMK (9) and AIADMK (8).

Smaller political parties—including Viduthalai Chiruthaigal Katchi (2 seats, 4.5%), Communist Party of India (1 seat, 2.3%), and Communist Party of India (Marxist) (1 seat, 2.3%)—retained representation through selective regional victories.

SECTION 3 · ST RESERVED CONSTITUENCIES

Tamil Nadu features two Scheduled Tribe (ST) reserved constituencies. In the 2026 election, these two ST constituencies were evenly shared between AIADMK, which secured victory in Yercaud (ST), and TVK, which won in Senthamangalam (ST).

Reserved constituencies continue to shape electoral representation across Tamil Nadu's democratic landscape.
