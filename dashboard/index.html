The records below detail the competitive breakdown of the election, including margin frequencies across all 234 seats, performance discrepancies inside reserved blocks (SC/ST), and the singular impact of postal returns.

Reserved Seat Competitiveness

Tamil Nadu sets aside 44 seats for Scheduled Castes (SC) and 2 seats for Scheduled Tribes (ST). Data indicates that reserved seats were substantially more competitive on average.

While General seats returned an average winning margin of 17,544 votes, SC seats had a tighter average of 14,192 votes. ST seats returned an ultra-competitive average of just 2,422 votes.
